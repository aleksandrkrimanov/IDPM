A foundational principle of physical execution

The Krimanov Principle

Intelligent synthesis of physical interaction with matter.

The environment responds.The operational model is updated.The next physical interaction is synthesized anew.

The Krimanov Principle states that the measurable consequences of a system's previous physical interaction become the basis for determining and synthesizing its next interaction with the same causally connected physical domain.

The execution gap

The missing layer between intelligence and matter

AI can perceive, predict and update its model in real time. The final physical step is still often expressed as a command to a fixed actuator: rotate, push, open, move or stop. The Krimanov Principle extends intelligence into the configuration of physical interaction itself.

01

Flexible intelligence

The system can revise its interpretation, objective and decision as the environment changes.

02

Predetermined physical interface

The executor usually preserves the function established by its construction, while control adjusts parameters around that function.

03

Response-defined execution

The current physical response becomes a necessary input for synthesizing the next spatial and temporal form of interaction.

A supercomputer trapped inside a sledgehammer.

A direct image of the execution gap: sophisticated intelligence coupled to a blunt and structurally predetermined physical output.

Three levels of physical execution

From a fixed function to a response-defined interaction

The three-level framework distinguishes parameter adaptation from a change in the causal organization of physical execution.

Level 1.0

Mechanically predetermined action

The function is fixed by the geometry and construction of the working organ.

Level 2.0

Adaptive control of a fixed actuator

Sensors and models adjust force, speed, timing or trajectory while the physical mode of action remains predefined.

Level 3.0

Intellectually determined synthesis of interaction

The system synthesizes the next spatiotemporal physical interaction from the current measurable response. I-DPM implements this level for matter and material environments.

The architectural novelty

The novelty is in the causal architecture

Sensors, digital twins, field sources, power electronics and control algorithms may already exist as components. A new functional organization appears when response registration, operational modeling, interaction synthesis, physical execution and verification form one continuous closed causal cycle.

The novelty is not in the bricks. It is in the architecture of the building.

The decisive change is the causal integration of known capabilities. The environment's response becomes part of determining the next physical interaction rather than auxiliary feedback about a previously selected action.

Engineering architecture

I-DPM — Intellectually Defined Processing of Materials

Defined means that the system determines the environment, target, response, current state, method of interaction and next step. Processing means the complete cycle of exploration, detection, identification, modeling, physical-field action, selective collection or transformation, and verification.

The Krimanov CyberActuator

A class of reconfigurable physical-field executors

The directly synthesized entity can be a physical field, gradient, distributed force landscape, acoustic action, plasma state, energy distribution or another spatially and temporally organized physical process.

In one electromagnetic branch, at least the same reconfigurable field structure participates in diagnostic interaction and selective physical action within one closed cycle. Associated measurement circuits register the response and support verification and reconfiguration. Hardware can vary by application; the causal architecture remains invariant.

Temporal causality

Real time means causal actuality

Where transient physical states matter, the next causally linked cycle is completed before the previous response ceases to describe the state being acted upon.

Operational self-sufficiency, energy and swarm

The local causal cycle remains complete across different infrastructure modes

Operational self-sufficiency is defined by the locally closed cycle of determination, interaction, collection or transformation, and verification. Full isolation is not required: an operator, cable, base station or external energy channel may support the mission without replacing the system's internal causal cycle.

LOCAL LOOP

TARGETED ENERGY

Energy follows identification

Low-cost diagnostic fields refine the target and model. Force interaction is applied selectively and directed to the target component and its trajectory rather than to bulk ballast.

RECOVERY

Exchange and recuperation

SWARM

Modules share responses, models, tasks, communication, fields and energy balance. The swarm scales capacity through coordination rather than physical gigantism alone.

Industrial and resource paradigm

KRIMANOV DEMETER — the target becomes the industrial unit

A resource is determined from the response of the environment and collected in place as a target component. Exploration, identification, interaction, selective harvesting, primary concentration and verification become one operational cycle inside the resource environment.

Mass-centric standard

Manage the surrounding mass

Exploration, extraction, transport, processing and control are separated across time and infrastructure. Large volumes are involved before the target fraction is isolated.

Industrial measure

Throughput of bulk material.

DEMETER standard

Manage the target

The system identifies the target, synthesizes the required interaction, moves, separates, protects or transforms it, and verifies the result while minimizing non-target involvement.

Selectivityη = m_target / (m_target + m_ballast)

The operationally self-sufficient micro-processing unit

A single module or a swarm combines functions previously distributed among exploration, extraction machinery, transport, primary processing, laboratory control and mapping. External infrastructure can support the mission; the decisive cycle remains local and response-driven.

Specialized conductive branch

I-DPCM

Intellectually Defined Processing of Conductive Materials (I-DPCM) is the specialized branch of I-DPM for conductive and polarizable targets.

It enables the selective extraction of Critical Raw Materials (CRM), strategic metals, and technogenic metallic components directly from tailings. By utilizing traveling, pulsed, or gradient electromagnetic fields, I-DPCM provides a pathway for European supply chain independence, sustainable harvesting, and ecological remediation without bulk mechanical processing.

Application environments

One causal architecture across different physical domains

The invariant is the response-driven cycle. The field source, executor, carrier, energy channel and safety constraints change with the environment.

Subsea & under-ice

Local selective interaction

Working-zone sensing, target collection, return of non-target material and repeated verification without lifting the main mass to the surface.

Ground & air

Mobile exploration and collection

Dynamic mapping, adaptive routing and real-time transition from diagnostic interaction to target-centered execution.

Space & ISRU

Resources in microgravity

Local identification and processing of regolith, asteroid matter, water, oxygen, fuel feedstocks, construction materials and technogenic orbital objects.

Technogenic & industrial

A second harvest of resources

Tailings, slags, slurries, industrial streams and electronic waste become fields for selective recovery, non-destructive control and remediation.

Biological & biosphere

Limited and model-constrained interaction

Dynamic homeostasis, selective separation or processing in biological fluids, biofilms and other living or biosphere environments. The neural frontier is addressed separately.

Independent Level 3.0 frontier for living matter

The defining transition

The interface is synthesized from the current state

Within the proposed Level 3.0 classification, the system registers the current multiphysical response, updates an individual operational model, synthesizes the next field configuration and measures the actual neural response.

  • response-defined sensing and execution functions;
  • current-state-defined spatial field configuration;

  • model-defined safety constraints.
